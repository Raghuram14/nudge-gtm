An **Engineering Operating System** is the layer that sits above fragmented tools. It does not replace GitHub or Jira. It normalizes their events into canonical entities, connects them in an Engineering Knowledge Graph (Context Graph), and produces Engineering Intelligence: answers to *why*, with evidence.

It is not an AI-powered analytics dashboard, another DORA platform, or a chatbot. Analytics are the foundation. The destination is evidence-backed decisions that humans still make.

The current product direction is honest and narrow: GitHub and Jira as design-partner sources, and a flagship investigation — why is this sprint at risk?
